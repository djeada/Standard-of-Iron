## Sounds we should have but do not

These cues are fired by the game already. They resolve to nothing, so they
play silence until an asset is added to the manifest and listed here.

### `ui.back`

- **Fires when:** Player leaves a screen or cancels a dialog.
- **Wanted:** Descending two-tone cancel blip, softer and darker than ui.click.

### `ui.tab_switch`

- **Fires when:** Player switches between tabs or side panels.
- **Wanted:** Short parchment slide, slightly brighter than ui.click.

### `ui.panel_open`

- **Fires when:** A panel, settings sheet or overlay opens.
- **Wanted:** Leather scroll unrolling, ~250 ms, rising.

### `ui.panel_close`

- **Fires when:** A panel, settings sheet or overlay closes.
- **Wanted:** Leather scroll rolling shut, ~250 ms, falling. Pairs with ui.panel_open.

### `ui.toggle`

- **Fires when:** A checkbox, switch or mode toggle changes state.
- **Wanted:** Small bronze latch click, two variants for on and off would be ideal.

### `order.move`

- **Fires when:** Move order issued to the current selection.
- **Wanted:** Short kit shuffle: leather, mail rustle, a single boot scuff.

### `order.attack`

- **Fires when:** Attack order issued against a picked target.
- **Wanted:** Sharp single horn stab, dry and close, no reverb tail.

### `order.patrol`

- **Fires when:** Patrol route confirmed between two waypoints.
- **Wanted:** Two-note horn phrase, calmer than order.attack.

### `order.stop`

- **Fires when:** Stop order cancels the current activity.
- **Wanted:** Single muted drum hit, abrupt cut.

### `order.hold`

- **Fires when:** Hold-position mode toggled on the selection.
- **Wanted:** Shields planting into dirt, one short thud.

### `order.guard`

- **Fires when:** Guard mode toggled on the selection.
- **Wanted:** Spear butt tapped twice on hard ground.

### `order.run`

- **Fires when:** Run mode toggled on the selection.
- **Wanted:** Quick kit rattle plus a short breath, suggesting a change of pace.

### `order.formation`

- **Fires when:** Formation placement mode entered or left.
- **Wanted:** Light standard pole shift, cloth snap.

### `order.formation_placed`

- **Fires when:** Formation ghost committed at the chosen position and angle.
- **Wanted:** Standard driven into the ground, single decisive thud.

### `order.gate_mode`

- **Fires when:** Gate command mode changes (open, close, auto).
- **Wanted:** Short iron bolt slide.

### `order.rally_set`

- **Fires when:** Rally point placed for a production building.
- **Wanted:** Small banner flap plus a peg driven into soil.

### `build.construction_started`

- **Fires when:** A builder begins work on a construction site.
- **Wanted:** Mallet on stake, saw start, brief crowd of work sounds. ~1 s.

### `build.construction_complete`

- **Fires when:** A construction site finishes and becomes a real building.
- **Wanted:** Timber settling into place plus a two-second warm confirmation phrase.

### `build.unit_queued`

- **Fires when:** A unit is added to a production queue.
- **Wanted:** Wax tablet stylus scratch, very short.

### `build.unit_ready`

- **Fires when:** A produced unit leaves the barracks.
- **Wanted:** Single low barracks bell. Must sit under the unit voice line that plays with it.

### `build.building_destroyed`

- **Fires when:** A building, wall segment or gate is destroyed.
- **Wanted:** Timber and masonry collapse with a rubble tail, ~2.5 s.

### `build.gate_open`

- **Fires when:** A wall gate swings open.
- **Wanted:** Heavy wooden gate on iron hinges, opening, ~1.5 s.

### `build.gate_close`

- **Fires when:** A wall gate swings shut and bars.
- **Wanted:** Gate shutting plus bar drop. Pairs with build.gate_open.

### `alert.objective_complete`

- **Fires when:** A mission objective is satisfied (for example an undead zone is cleared).
- **Wanted:** Short rising three-note brass figure, confident, ~1.5 s.

### `alert.objective_failed`

- **Fires when:** The mission is lost, failing its remaining objectives.
- **Wanted:** Short falling brass figure, dark. Mirrors alert.objective_complete.

### `combat.siege_launch`

- **Fires when:** A catapult or ballista releases.
- **Wanted:** Torsion release: rope creak, wooden arm slam against the stop, ~1 s.

### `combat.siege_impact`

- **Fires when:** A siege projectile lands.
- **Wanted:** Stone striking earth or timber with a debris tail, ~1.5 s.

### `combat.heal`

- **Fires when:** A healer restores health to an ally.
- **Wanted:** Warm, quiet, non-magical: water poured, cloth binding, a soft exhale.

  Wanted: A dedicated charge roar layered with running feet, faction-neutral.
